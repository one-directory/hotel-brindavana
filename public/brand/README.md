# WhatsApp glyph provenance

Official unmodified SVG files downloaded from the logo pack linked by [Meta's WhatsApp Brand Resource Center](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/) on 29 October 2026.

- `whatsapp-white.svg`: `01_Glyph/01_Digital RGB/03_SVG/Digital_Glyph_White_RGB_2026.svg`
- `whatsapp-black.svg`: `01_Glyph/01_Digital RGB/03_SVG/Digital_Glyph_Black_RGB_2026.svg`

Rendered at their original aspect ratio without recolouring, filters or geometry changes. White is used on dark buttons; black on the light header and gold event button. Source artwork is a WhatsApp trademark, not hotel artwork. Keep the label “WhatsApp” beside the glyph for text buttons.
